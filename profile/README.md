<h1 align="center">CRX</h1>

<p align="center">Institutional FX, settled on-chain. One desk, custom notionals, flexible tenors. 20+ pairs live.</p>

## What is CRX

> CRX is a matched-principal dealer: a smart contract holds pre-funded margin
> from a client and a liquidity partner, marks the position to a continuous
> Pyth feed, and settles it at expiry against the last-24h Pyth VWAP. It
> replaces the bank-intermediated non-deliverable forward (NDF) and the
> syndicate of dealer relationships behind it — onboard once, then trade FX
> with one counterparty.

## What CRX is

- **One desk, not a syndicate.** One onboarding, one network, instead of a separate relationship with every dealer.
- **CRX is your counterparty.** A matched-principal dealer — you see one price, CRX's own, not competing quotes.
- **Custom notional, flexible tenor.** Match the notional to the exposure; short-dated and bespoke tenors, not a fixed calendar.
- **Atomic settlement, 24/5.** On-chain settlement replaces T+2 through correspondent banks.
- **Non-custodial.** Margin lives in the contract, not on CRX's balance sheet.
